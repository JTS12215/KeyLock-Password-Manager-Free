Cryptographic Salt & Key Derivation

We utilize PBKDF2 key derivation with a unique salt for every vault. This adds a mathematical shield against Brute-Force attacks.

Zero-Knowledge Security

Your master password is the only key. We don't have a "reset" button because we never have access to your data.

Fernet (AES) Encryption

Your vault is locked behind industry-standard encryption, ensuring your data remains unreadable to anyone but you.

The KeyLock Mission

In an era where personal data is treated as a commodity, KeyLock was born from a simple necessity: Absolute Data Sovereignty.

Why Local-First?

KeyLock was born from a simple idea: Your data should never be a target. By removing the centralized commercial cloud, we remove the incentive for hackers. Your vault is your own. Whether you keep it on a local machine, thumb drive, Google or Dropbox, you are the only one with the key.
